About

A Life of Impact & Purpose

Pastor. Scholar. Visionary.

Pastor Yomi Okewunmi, fondly known as PY, is a dynamic minister, scholar, mentor, and author from Abeokuta, Ogun State, now based in Ibadan. He serves as Resident Pastor of SPAC Headquarters, Ibadan, where his teachings are known for their depth, clarity, and practical relevance.

With a First Class Honours degree in Philosophy from the University of Ibadan, a Distinction in his Master's, and a National Diploma in Insurance with Distinction from The Polytechnic Ibadan, PY embodies the spirit of excellence.

He is the visionary founder of Arise Conference & Eagles Convergence, Afrowecanism, Edutop Scholar, and Give Us This Month — movements that have empowered thousands across Nigeria and beyond.

Monthly youth gatherings now in their fourth year — prophetic meeting points for young people discovering their path in God, purpose in life, and place in destiny. This is to ensure that they take responsibility for Self, Family, and Generation.

A last-day crossover program into every new month. A program to commit the coming month into God's hands. There are testimonies of good news and full evidence of the Holy Spirit. Held live on our Facebook page.

Afrowecanism

An African ideology built on the powerful belief: "As Africans, We Can." Inspiring young people to embrace their African identity as a blessing, promoting confidence, excellence, and global impact.

Educational initiative helping students achieve academic excellence and unlock their highest potential. Many students have been mentored, guided, and empowered to succeed academically.
